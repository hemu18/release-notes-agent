# Exporting dashboards

Doc ID: DOC-05 · Source: Confluence · Last updated: 2026-06-10 · Audience: all customers

## Export formats
Dashboards can be exported as **PDF** or **CSV**.

- **PDF** keeps the charts and layout and is best for board packs and monthly reviews.
- **CSV** gives the raw numbers for further analysis in a spreadsheet.

## How to export
1. Open the dashboard.
2. Click **Export** in the top right.
3. Choose PDF or CSV and the date range.
4. The file downloads, or is emailed to you if it is large.

## Scheduling exports
Admins can schedule a weekly or monthly export under Reports > Scheduled exports.
