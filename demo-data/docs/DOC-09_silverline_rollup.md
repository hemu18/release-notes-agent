# Dealer-group roll-up view – Silverline Motors pilot

Doc ID: DOC-09 · Source: Google Drive · Last updated: 2026-11-14 · Audience: **Silverline Motors only — do not share with other customers**

## What we built
A roll-up view in reporting that combines the dashboards of several branches under one parent dealer group. Silverline asked for it to run their monthly dealer-group reviews in one place.

## How to use it
1. Open Reporting > Dashboards.
2. Choose **View: Dealer-group roll-up**.
3. Pick the parent group. Scores and response counts are combined across its branches, with a per-branch breakdown below.

## Limits during the pilot
- Enabled for Silverline Motors only.
- Roll-ups can be exported as CSV.
- No date has been set for making this available to other customers. Do not mention timing to anyone outside Silverline.
